# PlatinoHunter Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 8, 2026 · [Leer en español](../privacidad/)

PlatinoHunter is an Android app that helps you track your PlayStation Network trophies, find guides and discover what to platinum next. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, where it is stored and who it is shared with.

**In short:** the app has no servers of its own. Your data is stored only on your phone, and we don't sell or collect it. The app only connects to the third-party services it needs to work (Sony, the Steam store and guide websites).

> PlatinoHunter is an independent app. **It is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Sony Interactive Entertainment.** "PlayStation", "PSN" and game names and images belong to their respective owners.

## 2. What data the app uses and why

### 2.1 Your PlayStation Network account

- **Sign-in.** You sign in on Sony's official page, which opens in your browser (Chrome) or in an in-app browser. **The app never sees your password or passkey.** Sony gives the app an access code, which the app exchanges for a session token. You can also paste a session token (NPSSO) obtained from Sony's website.
- **Data the app gets from Sony:**
  - Your username (Online ID), avatar and trophy level.
  - Your game list, trophy progress and each game's trophies (names, descriptions, the date you earned them and their rarity).
  - Your PSN friends list.
- **Why:** to show your library and progress, and to build your recommendations, your platinum collage and your review cards.

### 2.2 Your PSN friends' data

To improve recommendations, the app checks which platinums your friends with **public profiles** have earned, and in what order. Private profiles are skipped.

- Their account ID is **not stored in plain text**: the app stores an irreversible digest (truncated SHA-256 hash) that cannot reveal who they are.
- For each friend, only which games they platinumed and when are stored. Their username and avatar are not stored.
- This data stays on your phone and is never sent anywhere.

### 2.3 Your reviews

The reviews you write (rating, "like" and text) are stored **only on your phone**. They only leave it if you choose to share the review image.

### 2.4 What the app does not collect

- No analytics or tracking tools.
- No third-party crash reporting.
- No access to your location, contacts, camera or microphone.
- No storage permissions: images are saved to your gallery through Android's system (MediaStore), without extra permissions.

**Android permissions:** only `INTERNET` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, to connect to the services described here and to know whether you are online.

## 3. Where data is stored

Everything is stored **on your phone**:

- **PSN session token:** encrypted with a key from Android's secure storage (Android Keystore).
- **Everything else** (your library, trophies, reviews, game data, guide index and friends' data): in a local database inside the app.
- **Backups:** Android automatic backup is disabled for the app, so this data is not uploaded to your Google account.

## 4. Third-party services the app connects to

The app connects directly to these services, always over HTTPS. Each has its own privacy policy and receives the usual technical information of a connection, such as your IP address or device language.

| Service | Purpose | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| **Sony (PlayStation Network)** · `ca.account.sony.com`, `m.np.playstation.com` | Sign-in, trophy, profile and friends data | Your session token and the requests for your data |
| **Steam store (Valve)** · `store.steampowered.com`, `api.steampowered.com` | Filling in game data (genre, estimated difficulty) | **Only game names**, never any of your data |
| **PlayStationTrophies.org** | Downloading its public guide index (once a week) | An anonymous request |
| **PSNProfiles, PlayStationTrophies.org** | Opening guides, only when you choose to | Opened in Chrome; what you do there is governed by those sites and by Google |
| **Image servers** of Sony and Steam | Loading covers, icons and avatars | Image requests |

Privacy policies: [Sony](https://www.playstation.com/legal/privacy-policy/) · [Valve/Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/) · [Google/Chrome](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. Advertising

The app may show **at most one ad per day**, served by **Google AdMob**. Google may collect and use device data, such as the advertising ID, IP address, and technical and ad-interaction data, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. It does so under its own policy: [how Google uses data](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

- In the **European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland** (and wherever the law requires it), the app asks for your **consent** before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the app's settings.
- You can reset or turn off your advertising ID in *Android Settings → Google → Ads*.
- PlatinoHunter **does not share** your trophies, reviews or any PSN account data **with AdMob**.

## 6. Sharing images

The platinum collage and review cards are created **on your phone**. They only leave it when you tap **Share** (you send them to the app you choose) or **Save** (they are saved to your gallery). Note that these images include your PSN username, avatar and trophies.

## 7. How long data is kept and how to delete it

Data stays on your phone until you delete it:

- **Logging out** (in *Settings*) deletes your session token, library, trophies and platinum history. Your reviews, game data, guide index and anonymous friends' data **are kept**.
- **To delete everything**, uninstall the app or use *Android Settings → Apps → PlatinoHunter → Storage → Clear data*.

Since we have no servers, **we keep no copy of anything** that we could look up or delete for you.

## 8. Your rights

Since all your data is on your device, you are in full control: you can view it in the app and delete it as explained above. If you live somewhere with data protection laws (for example the GDPR in the EU or Mexico's LFPDPPP) and have any question, write to the contact address.

For data handled by Sony, Valve or Google, exercise your rights directly with them.

## 9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and does not knowingly collect children's data. Using it requires a PlayStation Network account, which has its own age requirements.

## 10. Security

- All connections use HTTPS.
- The session token is stored encrypted.
- The app never handles your PSN password.

Still, no system is infallible: protect your phone with a screen lock.

## 11. Changes to this policy

If what the app does with your data changes, we will update this policy and the date above. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
